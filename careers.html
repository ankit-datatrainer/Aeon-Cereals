Life at Aeon

A small team with a large responsibility

For three decades our people have sourced, graded, tested and delivered food that Indian families trust. We are a close-knit team in Rohini, New Delhi, and every role here touches the product that reaches a household table.

We look for people who care about doing things properly: checking a batch twice, keeping a promise to a retailer, and telling the truth on a label. In return you get real ownership early, direct access to the founders, and the chance to help build 4K Natural into a national brand.

Current openings

What you'll do

  • Grow 4K Natural distribution across kirana, modern trade and institutional buyers.
  • Appoint and support distributors, and track secondary sales in your territory.
  • Negotiate listings, run visibility activities and manage collections.

What you bring

  • 2 to 4 years of FMCG or food sales experience in Delhi NCR.
  • A two-wheeler and a valid driving licence.
  • Fluency in Hindi and working English.

What you'll do

  • Inspect incoming lots, run in-house checks and release batches for packing.
  • Coordinate six-monthly testing with NABL or FSSAI-recognised laboratories.
  • Maintain ISO 22000 and FSSAI records, and support audits.

What you bring

  • A degree in Food Technology, Microbiology, Chemistry or a related science.
  • 1 to 3 years in food quality, ideally in grains, spices or dry fruits.
  • Working knowledge of FSSAI regulations.

What you'll do

  • Run inward, storage, packing and dispatch for trade and online orders.
  • Keep stock accurate, rotated and hygienically stored.
  • Lead the packing team and plan daily dispatch schedules.

What you bring

  • 3 or more years in food or FMCG warehousing.
  • Comfort with inventory software and daily stock reporting.
  • A steady, hands-on leadership style.

What you'll do

  • Plan and run social, search and marketplace campaigns for 4K Natural.
  • Create product content, photography briefs and customer communications.
  • Report on performance and improve conversion on the online store.

What you bring

  • 1 to 3 years in digital marketing, ideally for a food or D2C brand.
  • Hands-on experience with Meta and Google ad platforms.
  • A portfolio of campaigns or content you have worked on.

What you'll do

  • Manage billing, receivables, GST returns and vendor payments.
  • Reconcile distributor accounts and support the statutory audit.
  • Prepare monthly MIS for management.

What you bring

  • B.Com or equivalent, with 2 to 4 years in accounts.
  • Working knowledge of Tally and GST compliance.
  • Accuracy and discretion with financial information.
