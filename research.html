The R&TD mandate

Evidence before every decision

The Research & Technology Development function sets the scientific standard for everything that carries the 4K Natural name, from the grower's field to the sealed pack.

It writes the specifications our buyers source against, validates the cleaning, grading and packing methods used at our FSSAI-licensed facility in Rohini, develops new formulations, and decides what each product must be tested for before it is released.

  • Define raw-material and finished-product specifications for every line
  • Validate processes so that purity is built in, not inspected in
  • Develop clean-label formulations without artificial colour, flavour or preservative
  • Keep every product compliant with FSSAI standards and labelling rules

Focus areas

Six disciplines, one standard

Our research programme is organised around the six questions that decide whether natural food is also safe, consistent and good to eat.

01

Food safety & analytical science

Methods to detect what should never be in food: adulterants, mycotoxins, pesticide residues, heavy metals and microbial contamination.

  • Contaminants
  • Microbiology
  • Adulteration
02

Process technology

Cleaning, de-stoning, sorting, grading and packing methods that remove impurities mechanically, so nothing needs to be added chemically.

  • Cleaning
  • Sorting
  • Grading
03

Raw-material science

Grade specifications, varietal knowledge and grower standards that let our buyers recognise quality at the source, lot by lot.

  • Specifications
  • Varieties
  • Grower standards
04

Formulation & product development

Masala blends, herbal teas, mukhwas and wellness products built from recognisable ingredients, with flavour that comes from the ingredient itself.

  • Blends
  • Herbal teas
  • Wellness
05

Shelf-life & packaging science

Moisture, oxygen and light are the enemies of natural food. We study packs and storage conditions that keep products fresh without preservatives.

  • Barrier packs
  • Moisture control
  • Storage
06

Regulatory science

Interpreting FSSAI standards, labelling and claims regulations so that every pack says exactly what is inside it, and nothing it cannot prove.

  • FSSAI
  • Labelling
  • Claims
From field to release

Our quality pipeline

01

Source specification

Each raw material is bought against a written specification for grade, moisture, foreign matter and origin.

02

Incoming inspection

Every lot is sampled on arrival and held until it passes physical and sensory checks.

03

Process validation

Cleaning, grading and packing lines are verified to remove impurities without adding anything.

04

Release testing

Finished batches are checked against specification and coded for traceability before dispatch.

05

Market surveillance

Retained samples, shelf-life monitoring and customer feedback close the loop for the next batch.

What we test for

Parameters that protect your kitchen

Chemical and microbiological testing is carried out at NABL-accredited or FSSAI-recognised laboratories, as our licence requires, alongside routine in-house checks on every batch.

The parameters depend on the product. Oils and ghee are tested for purity and rancidity, spices for colour additives and contaminants, and grains, pulses and dry fruits for moisture, infestation and mycotoxins.
