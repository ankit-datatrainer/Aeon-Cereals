Chapter 011995
Modest beginnings

Mammoth aspirations

Founder-Chairman Late Shri Siya Ram Goyal establishes Aeon Cereals Limited, with Rajiv Goyal on its founding board. The company is incorporated with the Registrar of Companies, Delhi, as a public company limited by shares with an authorised capital of one crore rupees. The trade rewards volume; he bets instead on grain a household can trust.

Chapter 021998
A promise is made

One hundred per cent natural

The company commits to sourcing only what nature made: no artificial colour, no artificial flavour, nothing bleached or blended to disguise a poor lot. It is the founding rule that 4K Natural still prints on every pack today.

Chapter 032000s
From grains to the whole pantry

Widening the shelf

Spices, flours, dry fruits and edible oils are added to the cereals and pulses trade. Aeon grows from a single-category house into a wholesaler and distributor working across fourteen food categories.

Chapter 042010
Building the staples business

The everyday Indian kitchen

Masur, urad, moong, chana, rajma, lobia and moth bean join rice, oats and soyabean on the line. Atta, maida, suji, besan and whole maize flour follow, establishing Aeon as a dependable supplier across Delhi.

Chapter 052016
Strengthening governance

A board for the long term

Vandana Chharia joins the board of directors, bringing depth in finance and statutory compliance. The governance of a public company is put on a footing designed to outlast its founder.

Chapter 062018
The brand takes shape

Quality with a name on it

Bhawna Goyal joins the board with oversight of brand and quality systems. Packaging, batch records and testing are redesigned around what a shopper can see and verify.

Chapter 072022
Licensed and accountable

The FSSAI State Licence

The Department of Food Safety, Government of Delhi, grants a State Licence for the Rohini premises. Twenty-six manufacturing lines are approved at a capacity of one metric tonne a day.

Chapter 082023
Certified systems

ISO 9001 and ISO 22000

Quality-management and food-safety systems are certified across sourcing, grading, repacking, storage and distribution. Six-monthly laboratory testing becomes routine, not exceptional.

Chapter 092024
Direct to the household

4K Natural goes online

The consumer brand launches its digital store with eleven categories, carrying three decades of wholesale discipline directly into the household.

Chapter 102024
Growing without shortcuts

The organic tier

Certified organic oil, pulses, spices and wheat join the range, grown without synthetic fertilisers or pesticides. Organic is not a trend here; it is the logical conclusion of a promise made in 1995.

Chapter 112025
Thirty years, unbroken

A clean licence record

The State Licence is renewed for a third consecutive year through October 2025, with no suspension in its history, as the company invests in organic and direct-to-consumer lines.

Chapter 12Today
The next chapter

Purity is life

Three directors, one licensed facility and thousands of households. The road ahead runs through deeper organic and wellness ranges, clean-label foods and batch-level traceability.
