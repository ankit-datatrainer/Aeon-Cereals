Over thirty years of

A grain of an idea, grown into a house of trust

Nature holds the key to a healthier life. Our job is not to improve on nature, only to deliver it honestly.

Rajiv Goyal · Founder-Chairman, Aeon Cereals Limited
1995
1995 Modest beginnings

Mammoth aspirations

Rajiv Goyal incorporates Aeon Cereals Limited with the Registrar of Companies, Delhi, on 22 December 1995, as a public company limited by shares with an authorised capital of one crore rupees. The trade he enters rewards volume; he bets instead on grain a household can trust.

1998
1998 A promise is made

One hundred per cent natural

The company commits in writing to sourcing only what nature made: no artificial colour, no artificial flavour, nothing bleached or blended to disguise a poor lot. It is the founding rule that the 4K Natural brand still prints on every pack today.

2000s
2000s From grains to the whole pantry

Widening the shelf

Spices, flours, dry fruits and edible oils are added to the cereals and pulses trade. Aeon grows from a single-category house into a wholesaler and distributor operating across fourteen food categories, from dairy and bakery to sweeteners and ready-to-eat savouries.

2010
2010 Building the staples business

The everyday Indian kitchen

Masur, urad, moong, chana, rajma, lobia and moth bean join rice, oats and soyabean on the line. Atta, maida, suji, besan and whole maize flour follow, establishing Aeon as a dependable supply for Delhi's retail and institutional buyers.

2016
2016 Strengthening governance

A board for the long term

Vandana Chharia joins the board of directors, bringing depth in finance and statutory compliance. The governance of a public company is put on a footing built to outlast its founder, with committees taking responsibility between board meetings.

2018
2018 The brand takes shape

Quality with a name on it

Bhawna Goyal joins the board with oversight of brand and quality systems. The work of turning three decades of trade reputation into a consumer brand begins, with packaging, batch records and testing designed around what a shopper can verify.

2022
20 Oct 2022 Licensed and accountable

The FSSAI State Licence

The Department of Food Safety, Government of Delhi, grants State Licence 13322001000902 for the premises at Sector 9, Rohini. Twenty-six manufacturing lines are approved at a capacity of one metric tonne a day, with Rajiv Goyal named personally as the person responsible for compliance.

2023
2023 Certified systems

ISO 9001 and ISO 22000

Quality management and food-safety management systems are certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 22000, covering sourcing, grading, repacking, storage and distribution. Six-monthly laboratory testing at NABL or FSSAI-recognised labs becomes routine, not exceptional.

2024
2024 Direct to the household

4K Natural goes online

The consumer brand launches its digital store with eleven categories: dhoop and agarbatti, dry fruits, wellness and supplements, mukhwas, oils, ghee, pulses and rice, spices, tea and herbal tea, and organic products. Thirty years of wholesale discipline reaches the household directly.

2024
2024 Growing without shortcuts

The organic tier

Certified organic oil, pulses, spices and wheat join the range, grown without synthetic fertilisers or pesticides. For a company founded on the idea that nature needs no improvement, organic is not a trend but the logical end of a promise made in 1995.

2025
Oct 2024 – 2025 Thirty years, unbroken

A clean licence record

The State Licence is renewed for a third consecutive year through October 2025, with no suspension in its history. Net profit for the year ended March 2025 rises sharply as the company invests in its organic and direct-to-consumer lines.

Today
Today The next chapter

Purity is life

Three directors, one licensed facility and thousands of households. The road ahead runs through deeper organic and wellness ranges, a clean-label snacks line, and batch-level traceability so that every pack can say exactly where it came from.
