A journey that began with a grain.

From a cereals house in 1995 to a certified natural-foods enterprise, our story is one of patience, provenance and the discipline to never cut corners.

The beginning

Incorporated on 22 December 1995.

Aeon Cereals Limited was registered with the Registrar of Companies, Delhi, as a public limited company with an authorised capital of ₹1 crore. Its founding purpose was simple: bring clean, honestly graded cereals and pulses to Delhi's wholesale trade.

The company was built by Rajiv Goyal, who has served as a director continuously since incorporation. In an era when adulteration was the norm in loose grain markets, Aeon's discipline of sourcing from known growers and grading every lot set it apart.

Milestones

Three decades, one standard.

1995

Incorporation

Aeon Cereals Limited is incorporated in New Delhi. Rajiv Goyal joins the board as founding director.

1998

The natural promise

The company commits to 100% natural sourcing, the founding principle that the 4K Natural brand still carries on every pack.

2000s

From grains to the whole pantry

Spices, flours, dry fruits and oils are added. Aeon becomes a wholesaler and distributor across fourteen FSSAI food categories.

2016–18

Board strengthened

Vandana Chharia and Bhawna Goyal join the board, bringing the governance depth of a public company to a family-built enterprise.

2022

FSSAI State Licence

Licence 13322001000902 is granted for repacking, wholesale and distribution from Sector 9, Rohini. Twenty-six product lines are approved.

2024

4K Natural goes online

The consumer brand launches its digital store with eleven categories, ISO 9001 and ISO 22000 certification, and lab-tested batches.

Today

Purity is life

Three directors, one facility, thousands of households. The next chapter: organic staples and wellness for every Indian kitchen.
