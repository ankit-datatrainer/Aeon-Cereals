Who we are

Three decades of natural food, done properly

Aeon Cereals Limited is an unlisted public company registered with the Registrar of Companies, Delhi. What began as a cereals and pulses house in 1995 has grown into a multi-category natural foods enterprise.

Under our consumer brand 4K Natural, we repack, wholesale and distribute dry fruits, spices, flours, pulses, oils, ghee, teas and organic staples from an FSSAI-licensed facility in Rohini, North-West Delhi. Every batch is checked, not treated.

  • FSSAI State Licence 13322001000902
  • ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 22000 certified operations
  • Sourced from Indian growers, lab-tested batch by batch

Our promise

Everything nature intended. Nothing it didn't.

Our commitment is simple and it has not changed since 1995. We do not improve on nature, we deliver it honestly, and we can prove it batch by batch.

Chemical and microbiological testing is carried out at NABL or FSSAI-recognised laboratories at least twice a year, and our production, raw-material and sales records are maintained daily as our licence requires.
