Board Committees

Focused oversight between board meetings, with terms of reference drawn from the Companies Act, 2013 and the food-safety obligations of our FSSAI licence.

Structure

Five committees, one standard.

Each committee reports to the full board and is chaired by a director. Membership is reviewed annually at the board meeting following the Annual General Meeting.
