In remembrance

A life devoted to honest food

Late Shri Siya Ram Goyal founded Aeon Cereals Limited in New Delhi in 1995 with a conviction that shaped everything that followed: food should reach the family exactly as nature made it.

He began his working life in the grain and pulses trade of Delhi, where he learned to judge a lot by hand and to value a grower's word as much as a written contract. He saw how often quality was traded away for volume, and resolved to build a company that would never make that bargain.

Under his chairmanship, Aeon Cereals Limited earned a reputation among growers, traders and retailers for fair dealing, prompt payment and consistent quality. He insisted that every lot be graded and checked, that nothing be blended, bleached or coloured, and that a promise to a customer be kept whatever the cost.

Purity is Life: the principle Shri Siya Ram Goyal lived by, and the promise Aeon Cereals Limited still makes on every pack.The founding principle of Aeon Cereals Limited

A man of simple habits and deep faith, he was known for his humility, his generosity to those who worked with him, and his belief that a business exists to serve society. His vision continues to guide the company under the leadership of his family and the Board of Directors.

The values he left us

  • Purity before profit: deliver food exactly as nature made it.
  • Fairness to growers: pay a fair price, and pay it on time.
  • Respect for the customer: never sell what you would not serve at home.
  • Service to society: a business is measured by the good it does.
His legacy

What his vision built

A trusted company

Aeon Cereals Limited, a public limited company serving Indian kitchens for three decades from its home in Rohini, New Delhi.

A brand with a promise

4K Natural, which carries his rule of nothing artificial, ever, to dry fruits, spices, pulses, oils, ghee, teas and organic staples.

A commitment to society

The Aeon Research Foundation, which works in rural development, education, health, sports and women's empowerment in his spirit of service.

Carrying the vision forward
Leadership today

Rajiv Goyal, Chairman & Managing Director

Shri Rajiv Goyal has served on the board of Aeon Cereals Limited since its incorporation in 1995 and leads the company today as Chairman and Managing Director.

He oversees sourcing, quality and operations, is named as the person in charge of operations on the company's FSSAI licence, and chairs the Corporate Social Responsibility and Food Safety & Quality committees of the board. Under his leadership the company launched 4K Natural, its certified organic range and its online store.
