Dear stakeholders

Purity, provenance and the next thirty years

When we incorporated Aeon Cereals in 1995, India's food trade rewarded whoever could sell the most, fastest. We chose a slower road. Thirty years on, I am more convinced than ever that it was the right one.

Every product that carries our name, and the 4K Natural mark, passes through the same gate: is it exactly what nature made, and can we prove it? That is why we test every batch, why we buy directly from growers we know, and why our FSSAI licence names me personally as the person responsible for compliance. Accountability should have a face.

We are a small company with a large obligation. Families trust us with what they feed their children. There is no quarterly target worth more than that.Rajiv Goyal · Chairman & Managing Director

The year gone by has been one of consolidation and reach. Our State Licence was renewed in October 2024 across twenty-six manufacturing lines and fourteen wholesale and distribution categories. The 4K Natural online store now serves customers nationwide across eleven categories, and our certified organic tier has grown into oils, pulses, spices and wheat.

Looking ahead, we will deepen our organic and wellness ranges, bring our snacks line to market, and invest further in testing and traceability so that every pack can tell you where it came from. We will do this with the same board discipline that has kept our compliance record clean for three decades.

To our customers, growers, partners and colleagues: thank you for your trust. Purity is life, and we intend to keep proving it.

Rajiv Goyal

Chairman & Managing Director, Aeon Cereals Limited
