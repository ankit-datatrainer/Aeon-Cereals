Why the Foundation exists

Three decades of buying from Indian growers have taught us that the purity of food begins long before harvest. The Foundation exists to give back to the soil, the farmers and the families who make our work possible.

Mission

Strengthen the food system at its roots

To improve the livelihoods of the farmers we depend on, restore the health of the land they farm, and help families make informed choices about the food they eat, using research to guide every programme.

Vision

Pure food, fairly grown, for every Indian kitchen

An India where natural, safe and nourishing food is the norm rather than the exception, and where the people who grow it share fairly in its value.

About the Foundation

Where business knowledge meets social purpose

The Aeon Research Foundation brings together the company's sourcing relationships, its food-science expertise and its corporate social responsibility commitments under one roof.

It works where Aeon Cereals Limited has the most to offer: with growers and farming communities, on soil and water stewardship, on nutrition and food-safety awareness, and on research that helps natural food become safer and more widely available.

The Foundation is guided by the Corporate Social Responsibility Committee of the Board, which approves its annual plan and reviews progress on every programme.

Focus areas

Four commitments

Farmer livelihoods

Fair, direct buying relationships, training in good agricultural practice and support for growers moving to organic farming.

Soil & water health

Soil testing, composting, crop rotation and water-saving practices that keep farmland productive for the next generation.

Nutrition & food safety

Awareness sessions in schools and communities on balanced diets, reading labels and spotting common food adulterants.

Research & fellowships

Support for students and researchers working on food safety, post-harvest technology and traditional Indian foods.
