BoilerplateAbout Aeon Cereals Limited

Aeon Cereals Limited (CIN U74899DL1995PLC072664) is an unlisted public company incorporated in New Delhi in 1995. Through its 4K Natural brand it repacks, wholesales and distributes dry fruits, spices, pulses, flours, oils, ghee, teas and organic staples from an FSSAI-licensed, ISO 9001 and ISO 22000 certified facility in Rohini.
