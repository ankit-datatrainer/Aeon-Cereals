Why the Foundation exists

For three decades the farmers and families of rural India have made our work possible. The Aeon Research Foundation exists to give back, by helping villages grow stronger, children learn, families stay healthy, women lead and young people play.

Mission

Build opportunity where it is needed most

To improve the quality of life in rural and underserved communities through sustained programmes in livelihoods, education, health, women's empowerment and sports, designed with the community and measured by the change they create.

Vision

Every village healthy, educated and self-reliant

An India where a child's future, a woman's independence and a family's health do not depend on where they were born.

What we do

Five pillars of impact

Each pillar runs as a long-term programme with local partners, clear goals and regular review by the Board.

01/ 05
Pillar 01

Rural Development

Thriving farms make thriving villages. We work alongside farming communities to raise incomes, protect natural resources and improve the basic facilities that rural families depend on.

  • Training in good agricultural practice, soil health and organic farming
  • Fair, direct market linkages for growers and producer groups
  • Water conservation, tree plantation and clean-village drives
  • Support for village infrastructure such as drinking water and sanitation
Pillar 02

Education

Education is the surest path out of poverty. We help children stay in school, learn well and go further, with particular attention to girls and first-generation learners.

  • Scholarships for meritorious students from low-income families
  • School kits, libraries and smart-class support for government schools
  • After-school learning centres and digital literacy for youth
  • Food-science fellowships and mentoring by our R&TD team
Pillar 03

Health

Good health starts with good food and timely care. Our health programme brings both closer to families who would otherwise go without.

  • Free health check-up and eye camps in villages and urban settlements
  • Nutrition awareness and supplementary nutrition for children and mothers
  • Sessions on safe food, label reading and spotting adulteration
  • Support for hygiene, sanitation and menstrual health programmes
Pillar 04

Women's Empowerment

When women earn, families and communities rise. We help women build skills, start enterprises and take their place as decision-makers.

  • Formation and support of women's self-help groups
  • Skill training in food processing, packaging, tailoring and handicrafts
  • Opportunities to supply and work within the Aeon value chain
  • Financial literacy, digital payments and access to micro-credit
Pillar 05

Sports

Sport builds health, discipline and confidence. We help young people from rural and modest backgrounds play, train and compete.

  • Sponsorship and kits for promising young athletes
  • Village and school tournaments in kabaddi, kho-kho, athletics and football
  • Coaching camps with qualified trainers, open to girls and boys
  • Sports nutrition guidance from our food-science team

About the Foundation

Carrying forward a founder's spirit of service

Our Founder-Chairman, Late Shri Siya Ram Goyal, believed that a business is measured by the good it does. The Aeon Research Foundation carries that belief forward.

The Foundation brings together the company's corporate social responsibility commitments, its relationships with rural communities and the expertise of its people. It works through trusted NGOs, schools, hospitals, producer organisations and panchayats.

It is guided by the Corporate Social Responsibility Committee of the Board, which approves the annual plan, allocates funds and reviews the progress of every programme.
